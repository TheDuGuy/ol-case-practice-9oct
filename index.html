One last thing

AI as challenger, not decision-maker.

I used two models to generate options and challenge assumptions. The final decisions were mine.

TopicAI proposedI decided
Sizing~90k cohort from %s not in the brief→Only ~245k, from the brief
SegmentMirror-will couples as the core→A priority signal, written to individually
PersonalisationUse everything the will tells us→A sensitivity hierarchy, with some data left out
Operations"48 hours", "4–6 weeks"→Agreed SLAs and test hypotheses

Where it helped

  • Lots of options, fast
  • Stress-testing assumptions
  • Q&A preparation
  • Building this deck

Where it didn't

  • No knowledge of your data, ops or customers
  • Generic copy, invented numbers
Privacy, sensitivity and commercial trade-offs needed human judgement.
